# SquarePic GSC traffic decline investigation

Collected October 18, 2026, Asia/Calcutta. Property sc-domain:squarepic.io. Web search only. Latest returned daily row is October 14; October 15 onward is excluded. GSC dates use Pacific Time. All comparisons below contain equal numbers of complete days.

## Findings

The latest complete week, October 8 to 14 versus October 1 to 7, lost 33 clicks, down 31.1%, and 548 impressions, down 10.0%. CTR fell from 1.93% to 1.48% while average position improved from 9.42 to 8.42. Holding the previous CTR constant, lower impressions account for about 10.6 lost clicks and lower CTR for about 22.4. This is an arithmetic decomposition, not causal proof.

The www homepage accounts for 19 of the 33 net lost clicks; the non-www homepage loses another four. The upscaler loses six. For square pic, clicks fell 18 to 7, CTR fell 3.51% to 1.48%, and position weakened slightly from 5.40 to 5.87. India clicks fell 25 to 12 while impressions barely changed, 1086 to 1062, and position improved from 7.63 to 7.33. Mobile loses 23 clicks, 78 to 55, of the total 33. These observations make query and market-specific CTR weakness the clearest immediate issue, with modest rank slippage for square pic, rather than a broad ranking collapse.

The longer comparison is milder: 28-day clicks fell 418 to 402, down 3.8%, and impressions fell 28445 to 24058, down 15.4%. Desktop accounts for 3423 of 4387 net lost impressions, yet desktop clicks rose 94 to 118. This supports a substantial change in lower-value impression mix. The calculator loses 2384 impressions and the Reels guide 1295; their average positions improve sharply. Lower impressions do not translate directly into lost valuable traffic.

The exact brand query squarepic loses 40 clicks over 28 days, 94 to 54. Its impressions fall 212 to 155, CTR falls 44.34% to 34.84%, and average position shifts 1.70 to 2.05. Reduced branded search exposure and weaker click capture are both present. GSC alone cannot establish whether the lower exposure is demand, geography, competitors, or another SERP change.

Homepage, calculator, and upscaler all pass URL Inspection. The upscaler was last crawled October 17 with matching canonical and successful fetch. No sampled indexing failure explains the loss.

## Diagnosis and limits

The daily trend peaks in early/mid September and declines before the October 7 to 9 repository changes. Git commit dates are not deployment evidence. The September 2026 spam update ran September 24 to October 8; timing overlaps part of the decline but cannot establish causation. Source: https://status.search.google.com/summary

Aggregate position improves as impressions fall. This can reflect a changing mix of queries and fewer low-ranking impressions; it does not prove that every query improved. Mobile and individual square-image queries need separate treatment. Lower demand is plausible but not established without independent trend data. Competitor displacement, SERP changes, and AI-result effects are not proven by this export.

Homepage URL Inspection passes: indexed, crawl allowed, successful fetch, matching Google/user canonical, last crawl October 17. Calculator also passes, but its stored last crawl is September 21, before October edits. Thus those calculator changes cannot yet be credited with recovery or blamed for this decline based on this stored inspection. Live homepage, upscaler and Reels URLs return 200, permit indexing, and self-canonicalize. Root host redirects to www. These samples do not rule out all historical or sitewide crawl issues.

Submitted sitemap has zero errors and zero warnings and was downloaded October 15. Its indexed=0 field is not an indexed-URL count to interpret as deindexation; use URL Inspection. Manual Actions and Security Issues UI reports were not checked; the API evidence does not establish absence of a penalty.

## Recommended next steps

1. Prioritize the homepage queries square pic, squarepic and square image. Compare their device/country-specific rankings and live search snippets before changing titles or copy. Separate brand demand from generic square-photo intent.
2. Inspect calculator and Reels query losses individually. The calculator has stale stored crawl evidence; verify the latest deployed content and request indexing through GSC after confirming it is ready. No indexing request was made in this investigation.
3. Verify mobile loading and editor usability on the deployed site, especially for India. GSC measures clicks before users use the tool, so editor friction alone does not explain lower clicks.
4. Recheck complete seven-day windows when new data arrives. Avoid broad rollbacks based on the aggregate position or the partial final days.
5. Check Manual Actions, Security Issues and Page Indexing in the GSC UI before ruling out those causes.
