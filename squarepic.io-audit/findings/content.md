# Content quality audit

Audit date: 2026-10-08, Asia/Calcutta. Target: https://www.squarepic.io. No application files changed.

## Evidence and scope

The fresh crawl in `../data/crawl.json` contains 50 requested URLs, including the `/resize` redirect to the homepage. The sitemap contains 49 pages. This review covers the homepage, four image tools, calculator, 13 platform pages, 14 format-pair pages, nine guides, guide index, and five trust/support pages plus the author page. HTML, title, headings, descriptions, links, image tags and raw-response paths are saved per URL. Word counts in that file include site chrome and are descriptive, not quality thresholds.

The plugin renderer fetched `/upscaler` with `mode=auto` and rendered `/resize/instagram` with `mode=always`. The latter is saved in `../data/render-instagram-content.json`. It has no input elements after hydration; its resize CTA links to `/`. Web retrieval corroborated representative pages. The plugin runtime wrapper initially reported not ready; directly invoking its renderer worked. No assumptions about live deployment were made from local files.

Fresh GSC page and page-query exports cover September 8 to October 5, 2026, with August 11 to September 7 comparison. Use `../data/gsc/manifest.json` and the parent report for filters, extraction scope and totals. Query reports omit anonymized rows, so visible query clicks do not reconcile with all page clicks. Historical `docs/gsc-2026-10-07` was not used as current performance evidence.

## Assessment

Content quality: **53/100**, an internal editorial assessment, not a Google score or ranking prediction. The site has useful tools, clear ownership and real support routes. Its largest weakness is reliability: live copy often promises capabilities or platform certainty that the implementation and cited evidence do not establish.

| E-E-A-T factor | Internal score | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Experience | 11/20 | Working image workflows and public source link; no documented test corpus, original output comparisons, or platform preview screenshots in audited page HTML. |
| Expertise | 13/25 | Useful format/ratio explanations; unsupported resampling and recovery claims, imprecise format support and platform requirements reduce confidence. |
| Authoritativeness | 10/25 | Named development team and linked GitHub presence; independent reputation and backlink quality were not measured. This is provisional. |
| Trustworthiness | 19/30 | HTTPS, email, about, privacy and terms exist; privacy copy contradicts analytics disclosures, and advertised output types need validation. |

AI citation readiness: **45/100**, also an internal heuristic. Tables and question headings are easy to extract, but missing primary sources, incorrect platform rules and unsupported numerical claims make the extracted answers unreliable. No extra AI files or special content chunking are needed. [Google's guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) prioritizes helpful original content and standard SEO.

## Metadata, readability and duplication

The bundled deterministic metadata checker analyzed all 50 crawl records. Saved evidence: `../data/content-metadata-pairs.json` and `../data/content-metadata-template.json`. It returned `site_risk=low`, `templated_ratio=0.0`, `templated_count=0`, and no shared CTA phrases. This does not establish that every snippet is useful; it means that this particular detector did not flag templated metadata. Do not report a sitewide high-severity metadata-template defect.

The author title repeats the brand suffix on live. Platform titles lead with resizing, while H1s lead with dimension guides. The chosen primary intent should agree across title, H1 and actual interaction. Several long tool titles combine every adjacent use case; use the specific task, input/output and real benefit first.

Most prose is readable and organized into tables and short sections. The editing priority is removing repetitive and unsupported claims, not achieving a readability number. Formulaic headings and identical scaffolds are not evidence of AI authorship. No authorship detector was used.

The guides are already lengthy enough to contain the needed answers. They need documented corrections and original examples, not added generic paragraphs. Google explicitly says there is no magical word-count target in its [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

## Useful content to add, attached to real workflows

- Homepage: one original portrait and landscape comparison of Blur, Solid and Crop, including the export dimensions and whether content was trimmed.
- Upscaler: actual 2x and 4x exports of a photograph, small text and transparent artwork. Explain sharpening halos, memory use, alpha preservation and lack of AI reconstruction.
- Compressor: measured input/output bytes, chosen format, dimensions and a target-unmet example; show why a PNG may become JPEG/WebP and what happens to transparency.
- Converter: a tested matrix separating decode support from encode support. Explain static GIF output, lost animation, JPEG background, metadata/color-profile behavior and verified ICO sizes.
- Platform guides: original crop/safe-area overlays based on tested current previews, linked to the matching preset action. General placement advice should not be called a platform-enforced rule.

## Local work already present, but not verified live

Uncommitted local changes predate this audit. The local homepage and upscaler copy, calculator implementation, Discord guide/presets, Instagram/LinkedIn page specificity and contextual links already address parts of this report. The proposed no-crop guide exists locally but was not accessible through the live web retrieval. These are pending review/deployment, not new audit fixes and not confirmed live improvements. The local support/converter/compressor implementation risks remain.

Before marking any issue fixed, compare fresh live HTML, the actual editor state and an independently decoded download against the acceptance criteria. Record deployment revision and verification date. Use fresh GSC cohorts after recrawl to measure the effect; this audit cannot establish why traffic changed.
