These drafts are starting points, not a requirement to replace every existing title. Title length limits in local checks are house rules; Google truncates by available display width and can rewrite titles. Avoid cramming synonyms into titles. [Google's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) favors concise, descriptive, distinct titles.

## Opening copy for the first improvements

Homepage: "Fit a portrait or landscape photo into a square with a blurred or solid background. Keep the whole photo, or choose Crop to trim the edges and fill the frame. Edit and download in your browser without an account."

Upscaler: "Enlarge a PNG, JPG or WebP image 2x, 3x or 4x using browser smoothing and optional sharpening. This tool does not use AI or restore detail missing from the original. Choose PNG to retain transparency."

Calculator: "Enter width and height to calculate aspect ratio and megapixels. Set a new width to find the proportional height, then choose a matching image-editing preset. This calculates dimensions; it does not estimate a compressed file's size."

Reels/Stories guide: "Use 1080×1920 artwork as a starting point for a vertical Story or Reel cover. Check the actual placement preview before positioning text because controls and crop previews vary. SquarePic prepares still artwork; video duration and export settings belong to your video workflow."

Use these statements only after the corresponding behavior is verified live. A copy change cannot fix an incorrect download or an absent calculation.

## Page quality requirements

- Every tool page should let a visitor complete its named task and explain supported inputs, real outputs, limits, failure states and whether transparency/animation is retained.
- Every important platform specification should have a dated official reference or be qualified as a practical recommendation. Illustrations should use real tool results and explain what changes between fit, crop and resize.
- Every CTA from a task-specific page should retain the task and preset. A user searching for PNG-to-AVIF should not need to discover and reselect AVIF on a generic tool.
- Every guide should identify its actual author/reviewer and method of checking facts. SevenOneLabs should be typed consistently as an organization if the byline represents the lab.
- Keep related links specific. Instagram pages should link to the relevant Instagram guide, and Discord banner advice should link to the correct Discord workflow. Avoid uniform lists of every sibling on every page.

Functional tools are main content. Adding words to reach a quota will not compensate for an inaccurate result. [Google's helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) explicitly rejects a preferred word count.

## Validation and measurement

First verify actual output files and task state, then check live copy/metadata/schema after deployment. Track upload accepted, processing success/error and successful download without sending private image content or filenames to analytics. This audit has GSC traffic data but no GA4 conversion baseline.

After recrawl and 28 complete post-release days, compare the same page/query groups against the saved baseline, with device and country context where sample size permits. Keep unambiguous brand searches, ambiguous `square pic` terms and descriptive task searches separate. A fall in impressions with a rise in relevant clicks may indicate a healthier query mix; inspect the cohort rather than declaring success from one sitewide average.

If relevance work fails to increase useful clicks or task completion, inspect the competing result types, accuracy of the promise and the user steps before adding more pages. No keyword-volume, difficulty, controlled Google top-ten overlap or conversion-uplift estimate was collected.
