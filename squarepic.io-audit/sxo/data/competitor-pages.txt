L35: # Square Image: Make Any Image Square Online
L36: ## Short answer
L37: SquareImage.dev makes any photo square in the browser, free, with no account, no watermark and no upload: the image is processed on the device with Canvas. Four methods cover every case — a blurred background or a solid colour keeps the whole photo visible, a resize stretches it to 1:1, and a manual crop selects the region to keep. It accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP, and exports PNG, JPEG or WebP at 1080 x 1080, 1200 x 1200, the original edge length, or any custom square from 1 to 10,000 pixels.

L40: ## Square the canvas, not the photo
L41: 
L42: Cropping is the obvious way to get a square image — but it costs you pixels. A full-body portrait loses feet or a head, a landscape loses its sky, a poster loses its title. This tool takes the opposite approach: it keeps your entire photo and grows the canvas instead, padding the shorter sides with a background until the image is a perfect 1:1 square.
L43: The photo is never stretched or squashed — its proportions stay exactly as shot. You choose what fills the padded space: clean white for a gallery look, black for photography, a brand color picked with the color wheel, true PNG transparency for logos, or a cite6†blurred background fill for the soft social-feed look.
L44: Like every tool on this site, the conversion runs entirely in your browser with the Canvas API. Your image is never uploaded, and the export keeps the original resolution — the square's size is simply your photo's longest side.
