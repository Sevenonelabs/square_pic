Make an Image Square Online

Use this free square image maker when you need a 1:1 photo for Instagram, profile pictures, thumbnails or product listings. Choose Fit whole image to keep the full photo with a square background, or Crop to square to trim the frame to a perfect square.

Crop Image to Square

Crop mode locks the frame to a 1:1 aspect ratio. Drag the image inside the square and zoom until the important part of the photo is centered, then download the result as a PNG.

This is the right choice when you want a tighter composition, need to remove empty edges, or prefer a classic cropped square photo.

Fit a Photo Without Cropping

Fit mode keeps the entire image visible. The tool centers your photo on a square canvas and fills the remaining space with a background you choose:

  • blurred version of the image;
  • white or black;
  • a gradient sampled from the photo;
  • a custom color.

This works well for portrait or landscape photos that would lose important content if cropped, especially for Instagram posts where you want the full subject visible.

Square Photos for Instagram and Social Media

Many social platforms display square or near-square media prominently. A 1:1 image avoids awkward automatic cropping in feeds and grids.

  • Use crop when the subject should fill the square.
  • Use fit when the full photo matters more than edge-to-edge coverage.

The downloaded PNG can be uploaded directly to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, marketplaces and other apps that accept square images.

Supported Formats and Output

Upload JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP images. The finished square image downloads as a PNG so edges, text and flat backgrounds stay sharp.

Output dimensions depend on your source image:

  • Fit mode uses a square canvas sized to the longest side of the original image.
  • Crop mode uses the pixel dimensions of your selected 1:1 crop.

Private Browser Processing

Your image is read and processed locally in the browser. It is not uploaded to Fileverter's servers. This is useful for personal photos, client work and draft social content.

Very large images can require more browser memory. If processing is slow or fails, try a smaller file or close other tabs.

FAQ

How can I make an image square online?

Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image, then select Fit whole image to add a square background without cropping or Crop to square to choose a 1:1 crop. Preview the result and download it as a PNG.

Can I make a photo square without cropping it?

Yes. Fit whole image preserves the complete photo and fills the extra square space with a blurred, white, black, gradient or custom-color background.

Can I crop an image to a perfect square?

Yes. Crop to square provides a locked 1:1 frame. Drag and zoom the image to choose the part you want, then download the selected crop as a square PNG.

Can I make a square photo for Instagram?

Yes. Both modes create a 1:1 image suitable for square social posts. Fit mode is useful when you want to preserve the complete portrait or landscape photo instead of trimming its edges.

Which image formats are supported?

The square image maker accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images and downloads the finished square image as a PNG.

Are my photos uploaded or stored?

No. Image reading, cropping, background rendering and PNG generation happen locally in your browser. Your photo is not uploaded to Fileverter's servers.

Is there an image size limit?

There is no fixed upload limit in the tool, but very large images require more browser memory. If processing fails, try a smaller image or close other browser tabs.

Can I make multiple images square at once?

The tool currently processes one image at a time so you can review each crop or background before downloading it.

Does the tool stretch or distort my photo?

No. Fit mode centers the original image at its existing proportions, and crop mode removes content outside the selected 1:1 frame without stretching the image.
