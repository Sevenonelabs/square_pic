Crop or fit: two ways to make a square image

Every photo becomes a square one of two ways. Pick the mode that keeps what matters most in your image.

Crop to square

Cut a 1:1 region out of your photo. Drag to reposition and zoom in or out to frame exactly what you want, then download a tight, fully-filled square with no borders.

Best for social posts, avatars and thumbnails where the square should be completely filled.

Fit to square

Keep the entire photo and pad the shorter sides with a background color until the canvas is a perfect square. This is how you make an image square without cropping anything out.

Best for product shots, logos and artwork where every edge of the image needs to stay visible.

What size should a square image be?

A square image is any image with a 1:1 aspect ratio, meaning its width and height are equal. The best pixel size depends on where you use it, but 1080 × 1080 is the most common all-purpose size for social media.

Each platform has its own sweet spot: Instagram and Facebook show square posts at 1080 px, profile pictures are typically 400 px, and marketplaces like Etsy and Amazon want larger product squares. Because every export here keeps your source resolution, you can make one high-resolution square image and reuse it everywhere. For the full platform chart, see the square image sizes guide.

The 1:1 ratio is one of the standard image aspect ratios used across photography and design. Square Image keeps your original resolution, so you can export at whatever size your source image allows.
