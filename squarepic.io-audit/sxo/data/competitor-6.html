Crop an image to square or add padding?

Making an image square is not the same as forcing its width and height to match. Pick the result you want, and the editor will preserve the original proportions so your photo never looks stretched.

The difference at a glance

A crop fills every corner of the square but removes content from the longer sides. A fit keeps the complete image and uses a background to fill the rest of the 1:1 canvas. Both create a true square; the right choice depends on what cannot be lost.

Square image questions, answered

Quick answers about square crops, padding, image size, quality, and privacy.

1

How do I make an image square without cropping?

Choose Keep the whole image. The editor fits your complete photo inside a 1:1 canvas and fills the remaining space with a blurred background, a color, or transparency.

2

How do I crop an image to a square?

Upload or paste your image, choose Crop to square, then drag and zoom to frame the part you want to keep. Download the result at the original, preset, or custom square size.

3

What is the difference between cropping, padding, and resizing?

Cropping removes content from the longer sides. Padding keeps the whole image and adds canvas space. Resizing changes the pixel dimensions of the finished square. This tool preserves the photo’s proportions, so it does not stretch it.

4

What size is a square image?

Any image with equal width and height has a 1:1 aspect ratio, whether it is 512 × 512, 1080 × 1080, or another size. Choose the dimensions required by the site or project where you will use it.

5

How can I keep my square image from looking blurry?

Avoid exporting larger than the source detail available for the crop or fit.

6

Should I download JPG or PNG?

JPG usually creates a smaller file for photographs. Choose PNG when you need transparent padding. If you select JPG with a transparent background, those areas become white.

7

Is this square image maker free?

Yes. You can make and download square images without creating an account, and the exported image does not include a watermark.

8

Are my images uploaded or stored?

No. Your image is previewed and exported with browser image and Canvas APIs on your device, so it is not uploaded for processing.
